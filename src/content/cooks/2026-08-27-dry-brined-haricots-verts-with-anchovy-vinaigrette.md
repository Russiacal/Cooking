Made these alongside köfte and balela. I wanted to test dry-brining, because salt on top has never made green beans taste seasoned inside.

I salted the trimmed beans and left them for an hour, then boiled them to tender rather than crisp-tender and skipped the ice bath. I was skeptical, but they really did come out significantly more flavorful, and I will use that again. The vinaigrette is The Modern Proper's, without the white beans, tomatoes, or capers. It didn't do it for me. I think I put in too much Dijon mustard, so the anchovy didn't really come through, and even though I put it on when the beans were still hot, it didn't feel like it was doing much for them.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb haricots verts, dry-brined 1 hour
- 4 anchovy fillets
- 1 shallot (in place of ½ red onion)
- Dijon mustard (in place of stone-ground)
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- ⅓ cup olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, grated

## Next time
- Different approach to the dressing, with less Dijon.
