I've been trying to come up with summery dishes that still have protein but make the most of the good produce just now. Soupe au pistou came out of a round of summery bean dishes I was looking at a few weeks ago. I made the David Lebovitz version which just uses water -- no adding sausages or pancetta or anything like that.

I simplified it a bit and used two cans of butter beans instead of dried white beans (didn't have 12 hours of soaking time) and added with their liquid to thicken the soup. For the alliums I used one onion and one leek rather than one or the other, and added a fennel bulb, all of which went in right at the start together. I ended up just using whatever pasta shapes I had on hand -- mixing a couple of different types from various ends of boxes. They REALLY soaked up the liquid and swelled so next time I might cook them separately and add them at the end but only if I'm trying to preserve their shape / keep a bit of al dente bite on them.

I used store-bought pesto in place of pistou and it worked just fine. I found myself using a lot of pesto and still adding more salt and pepper towards the end. Not necessarily a bad thing but it wouldn't have been complete without it. A drizzle of good olive oil on top was also a nice touch along with some (a lot) of good bread.

## Ingredients
- 2 cans butter beans (in place of 1 cup dried white beans)
- 1 onion, 1 leek, 1 fennel bulb
- 2 carrots
- 2 zucchini
- 8oz green beans
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 cup peas
- 1 cup small pasta
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper
- Store-bought pesto (in place of pistou)
