Made this alongside köfte and dry-brined green beans.

Couldn't find sumac and forgot about the bell pepper as I was rushing, also used early girls and a regular cucumber, and didn't deseed anything as didn't have time. It was lacking a little bit of flavor and the chickpeas didn't feel incorporated somehow. It kind of had a tabbouleh with chickpeas vibe which was fine but not mind blowing. The beans themselves didn't take on enough flavor. I'll see what it tastes like the second day around and might add in more lemon juice and zest to see if that punches it up a bit. Next time might also leave the chick peas to sit in a lemon/salt brine first and probably will wash them to remove the aquafaba so they absorb more of the dressing.

## Ingredients
- 2 cans chickpeas
- 2 early girl tomatoes, diced
- 1 cucumber, diced
- Scallions, parsley, mint
- Olive oil, lemon juice and zest
- 1 clove garlic
- Salt, pepper
