Made these for a weeknight dinner with dry-brined green beans and balela (a Lebanese chickpea and herbs salad). I used Özlem's Kofte 101 as the base because I liked the two meat mix (lamb and beef) but took the spice blend from RecipeTin Eats' lamb koftas as I wanted something a bit more flavorful.

I didn't have cinnamon on hand, so didn't include it in the first batch and I think I underseasoned it with salt. The spice mix was pretty great -- though when I added cinnamon to batch 2 it did make a difference (I also added more salt). I didn't chop the parsley fine enough so some bites felt a little unincorporated, and there were some stems that I would probably go through the effort next time to remove entirely. I made greek yogurt with olive oil, lemon and salt to go with them and that was the real winner -- bright and zingy and balanced the spice mix in a nice way. Overall nice, and a good alternative to the Greek meatballs I normally make (with feta). Will def make again.

## Ingredients
- 225g ground beef, 225g ground lamb
- 1 medium onion, grated
- Stale bread, soaked and squeezed dry
- 1 egg
- 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
- 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp ground coriander, 2 tsp paprika, 1.5 tsp cinnamon
- Salt, black pepper
- Olive oil for shallow frying

## Next time
- More salt.
- Add the cinnamon.
- Spend more time mincing the parsley and remove the stems completely
