Made this again — the Daen's Kitchen version, the one where you fry the prawn heads and shells in the oil first to build the flavor. I had a bit over a pound of really nice head-on prawns.

Cleaning them to get the shells for the sauce was kind of annoying, mostly because Milo kept asking to be picked up while I had shrimp brain all over my hands. The oil flavor really is delicious.

I want to try it again using just paprika instead of the shells and see how much of a difference it makes. I still can't quite get the flavor right compared to what I remember eating in Portugal or Spain though — like something is missing. Might read up on some other recipes to see if there's a way to boost it even more.

## Ingredients
- A bit over a pound of head-on, shell-on prawns
- Olive oil (lots)
- Garlic (lots), sliced
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Salt
