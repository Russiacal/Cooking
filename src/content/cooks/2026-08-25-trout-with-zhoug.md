Whole Foods was selling butterflied trout, so I made zhoug to go on it.

I used the Tuesday Night Mediterranean recipe for the zhoug, which was awesome. I made it with one jalapeño instead of two serranos. The recipe is written for cod, snapper or tilapia, and I used trout.

I served it with sabzi polo. The pairing worked really well with the zhoug once we finished the fish.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups lightly packed fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems (about 1 large bunch), roughly chopped
- 2 medium garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
- 1 jalapeño, stemmed, halved and seeded (in place of 2 serrano chilies)
- 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
- Kosher salt and ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup plus 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- Butterflied trout (in place of four 6-ounce skinless cod, snapper or tilapia fillets)
- Lemon wedges, to serve
