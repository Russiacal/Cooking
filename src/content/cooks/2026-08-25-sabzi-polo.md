I made this as a side for butterflied trout with zhoug and wanted something that would go with it.

I used the Kitchn recipe halved, with 1 cup of rice instead of 2. I simplified the herb mixture significantly and used only dill. I parboiled for 6 minutes, cooked for 35, and rested for 5, in a 10-inch HexClad pan with the lid on. The bottom came out perfectly crispy.

Milo loved the crispy bits. Kai was mostly indifferent to it. I might make it again if we have company, as it's an easy wow dish.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup basmati rice (half the recipe's 2 cups)
- 1 cup dill (in place of the dill, parsley, cilantro, chives, and fenugreek mixture)
- 1/4 tsp saffron
- 1 1/2 tbsp kosher salt in the parboiling water
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
